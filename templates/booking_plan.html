Booking Plan

Use this checklist immediately before the official booking process.

  1. Confirm journey date and route.
  2. Confirm Tatkal quota and class.
  3. Check passenger names, age and gender.
  4. Keep your authorized railway account ready.
  5. Complete CAPTCHA/OTP yourself when requested.
  6. Complete payment through the authorized payment flow.
